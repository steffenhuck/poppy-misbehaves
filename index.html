How to be bad

  • Tap anywhere in the room and Poppy runs there. Arrow keys work too.
  • The big button does the crime. Some crimes you hold (tugging, chewing). One you can't stop once it's started.
  • They're out. Nobody sees what she does. But they come home at a moment you don't know.
  • Listen. A car, then the gate, then the key in the door. When you hear the car, it's them.
  • When the door opens, be in your bed. Asleep, of course. Tap To bed and she runs there.
  • Once she's done what she came to do, she can curl up and wait for them.
  • Every crime brings joy. And something else, which she'll find out about at the end.

Three rounds, in order. Out of bed when the door opens means starting again from the rug.
